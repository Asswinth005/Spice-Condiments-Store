01 — Origins

A story that began in a small kitchen in Kerala and grew into a global spice atelier.

It started with a single
mortar and pestle.

In 1987, our founder Rajan Menon began grinding small batches of garam masala for his neighbourhood in Fort Kochi. He had one rule: never compromise on the whole spice.

Word spread quickly. What began as a humble side project soon became a full-fledged spice atelier — one that refused to industrialise, refused to add fillers, and refused to let tradition die.

Today, we work directly with 28 family farms across Kerala, Kashmir, and the Malabar Coast. Every harvest is traceable, every batch is hand-mixed, and every jar carries the same promise Rajan made four decades ago.

We're still small. We're still slow. And we wouldn't have it any other way.

Rajan Menon

Founder, SPICEORA

02 — What We Stand For

Values we
refuse to grind down.

01

Purity First

No fillers, no anti-caking agents, no artificial colours. Ever. Just the whole spice, ground fresh.

02

Direct Trade

We buy directly from 28 family farms, paying fair prices and building multi-generational relationships.

03

Slow Craft

Cold stone grinding, woodfire roasting, hand blending. Machines are faster — but they're not better.

04

Traceability

Every jar carries a batch code. Scan it, and you'll know the farm, the harvest date, and the hands that made it.

05

Zero Waste

Spent spice husks become compost. Packaging is 100% recyclable. Nothing goes to landfill.

06

Made with Soul

Every batch is still tasted by a human. If it doesn't sing, it doesn't ship.
