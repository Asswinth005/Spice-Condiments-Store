From the Atelier Kitchen

Techniques that
change everything.

Small, deliberate details that separate good cooking from unforgettable cooking.

01

Tempering (Tadka)

Bloom whole spices in hot oil for 30 seconds. The fat carries flavour to everything it touches.

02

Dry Roasting

Toast whole spices in a dry pan until fragrant — usually 1–2 minutes. Never let them smoke.

03

Grinding Fresh

Pre-ground spices lose aroma within weeks. Grind small batches and store in airtight glass.

04

Layering Heat

Add spices at different stages. Early for depth, late for aroma. Both matter.

Chef's note

"When a recipe calls for garam masala, add half during cooking and half at the end. The first half builds the base; the second half perfumes the final dish." — Rajan Menon
